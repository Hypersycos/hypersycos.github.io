## Summary

### Summary of Game

Rogueframe (better name pending) is a WIP vertical slice of a co-op class-based horde shooter. It aims to provide lots of mechanical character customisation alongside varied objectives, allowing for players to assume roles in groups and tailor their win conditions to their preferred playstyles. The concept is loosely inspired by systems from Risk of Rain 2 and Warframe.
